Why the order comes out this way

Every object drops the same height, so each releases the same energy per kilogram. At any point:
mgh = ½mv² + ½Iω²,   with ω = v / r
Writing I = k·mr² gives
a = g sin θ / (1 + k)
  • When more mass sits far from the axis, k is bigger, so more of the energy goes into spinning and less into moving forward.
  • Mass and radius cancel out. Only the shape (k) matters.
  • The block doesn't spin (k = 0), so all of its energy goes into moving forward.
