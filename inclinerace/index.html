Rolling Race: How Moment of Inertia Sets the Pace

Five objects start from rest at the top of identical ramps and race 2.0 m to the finish line.

The block slides down a frictionless track, so it doesn't spin. The solid disk, hollow cylinder, solid sphere and hollow sphere roll without slipping. Static friction makes them spin, but it does no work, so no energy is lost.

All five have the same mass (1.0 kg). The four round objects all have the same radius (0.10 m), and the block is 0.20 m across. The only difference is how each object's mass is spread out, which sets its moment of inertia.

Predict: in what order will they cross the finish line?
