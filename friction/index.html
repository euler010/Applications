Contact Junctions and Asperities: Even smooth surfaces have jagged microscopic peaks called asperities. The colored circles mark real-time physical contact between the block and the table:
Orange Circles (Static Regime):

Deep interlocking contact junctions ("cold welds") pressed together by the normal force. Under pulling tension, they grip and resist motion until shearing at fs,max.

Green Circles (Kinetic Regime):

Fleeting, glancing contact points. As the block slides, it rides up onto asperity peaks with fewer simultaneous contact points, producing a lower constant resisting force (fk).
