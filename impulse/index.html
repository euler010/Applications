What is the coefficient of restitution?

The coefficient of restitution, e, measures how bouncy a collision is. For a ball hitting a wall, it is the rebound speed divided by the speed it hit with: e = |vf| ÷ |vi|. When e = 1, the ball leaves as fast as it arrived and no kinetic energy is lost (perfectly elastic). Smaller values mean a slower rebound, with energy turned into heat, sound and deformation; the fraction of kinetic energy kept is e². A bouncier ball changes its momentum more, so it needs a larger impulse from the wall.
