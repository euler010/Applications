How Cellular Radio Waves Work

1. Accelerating Charge: High-frequency alternating electric voltage forces conduction electrons to accelerate up and down the broadcast antenna (acceleration a ≠ 0).

2. Omnidirectional Waves: The oscillating electric field creates a coupled magnetic field (E ⟂ B), radiating outward across space in all directions at the speed of light c (≈ 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s).

3. Reception & Information: When the wavefronts arrive at the smartphone's internal antenna, the electric field exerts force (F = q · E), setting up an oscillating electric current that the smartphone demodulates into digital packets and clear audio!

Radio Waves Simulation Guide

Interactive Features:
  • Transmitter Power: Toggle the transmitter ON or OFF to observe how fields originate and stop.
  • Spreading Wavefronts: Observe the waves radiating across the entire space in all directions away from the broadcast mast.
  • Manual Wiggle: Switch to Manual Wiggle mode, grab the conduction electron on the mast, and move it up and down to create custom electromagnetic wave pulses.
  • Voice / Data: Modulate the carrier wave with digital packet data and observe how the phone demodulator decodes the stream. The tower transmits a spoken explanation of digital data transmission: watch each word leave the tower as a burst, and hear the phone say it as the burst arrives. The volume follows the signal strength.
  • Distance & Delay: Move the distance slider to observe transmission delay (Δt = r / c) and signal attenuation (1/r).
