Average acceleration between the two positions:

a =
Δv Δt
=
v₂ − v₁ Δt

Head-to-tail vector subtraction attaches −v₁ (vector v₁ inverted 180°) to the tip of v₂. Dividing the resultant Δv by scalar Δt yields acceleration a.

Observe that a has the exact same direction as Δv, but scaled by 1/Δt. As Δt → 0, a points straight toward the center!
