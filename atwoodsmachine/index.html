System Equations

When the system is in motion, the acceleration (a) and the dynamic tension (FT) are calculated as:

Acceleration: a = g · (m₁ - m₂) / (m₁ + m₂)
Tension: FT = (2 · m₁ · m₂ · g) / (m₁ + m₂)

When a mass rests on the ground, the system is in static equilibrium. The tension equals the weight of the suspended mass, and the grounded mass experiences a normal force (FN):

Suspended: FT = msusp · g
Grounded: FN = Fg - FT
