Thrower's eye

Camera tracks the ball
Floor grid lines are 8 m apart along the rim. The floor curves up ahead and behind.

In the outside view the ball travels in a straight line, because nothing pushes on it once it leaves the hand. Seen from inside the station, the same flight bends sideways: the Coriolis effect. Throw spinward and the ball lands short and stays low. Throw antispinward and it seems to float.
