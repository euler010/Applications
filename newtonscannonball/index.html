Newton's Thought Experiment (1728)

In A Treatise of the System of the World, Sir Isaac Newton demonstrated that planetary orbital motion and projectile free-fall are governed by the same universal law of gravitation.

At horizontal velocity v = √(GM/r) ≈ 7.35 km/s, the cannonball falls toward Earth at the exact rate the surface curves away underneath it.
