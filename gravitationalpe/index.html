Gravitational work and potential energy

Drag masses to begin.

m1 = 5.0 × 1024 kg, m2 = 1.0 × 1024 kg, G = 6.67 × 10−11 N·m²/kg². As the masses separate, gravity does negative work. Carried all the way to infinity, Wg = −Gm1m2/r0, which is the potential energy U0 of the starting arrangement (with U = 0 at infinity).
